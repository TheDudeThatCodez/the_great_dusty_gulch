Walking there now

The Great Dusty Gulch Christmas Caper

Christmas Eve. The Town Fund is gone. The law is in three pieces.

A note from the sheriff

Somebody robbed the saloon safe and took the Christmas Fund, every last coin meant for presents, pies, and the town tree. Then they ambushed me and snapped my badge in three. Without it, there ain’t no law in Dusty Gulch and nobody can clear a name.

Those three posters on the wall are of folks I know are being framed. Find my badge pieces, prove their alibis, and unmask the real varmint before midnight. Make it right, and I’ll deputize the lot of you.
— The Sheriff (excuse the penmanship)

Check the wanted posters and the map on the wall. Three stops, three alibis, three pieces of the badge. Start at the saloon.

9 PM: The Saloon

Aces Dalton deals the cards here, and she saw something she can’t explain. She’ll only talk to folks who’ve played a hand with her. Rebuild the winning hand from the night of the robbery.

When she’s satisfied, she’ll hand you a telegram. Type its code below.

11 PM: The Jailhouse

Locked. Clear the saloon first.

11 PM: The Jailhouse

Clue 1, from the saloon: A tuft of fur by the table. A card with a hole the size of a claw. And the dealer swears she “saw nobody.”

One of the posters says that suspect was locked in the jailhouse all night. The sheriff’s evidence log would prove it, but it’s in the evidence locker, and the key went missing when the badge broke. You’ve got the pick kit from the saloon.

The prisoner in the next cell knows a thing or two about locks. Listen to her. When the locker opens, you’ll find a new telegram inside.

Midnight: The Shooting Range

Locked. Open the evidence locker first.

Midnight: The Shooting Range

Clue 2, from the jailhouse: Tiny muddy paw prints on the windowsill. The sheriff’s note reads: “Whoever it was, they landed on their feet.” And the prisoner saw a little fella with a tail sneaking by.

The sheriff never trusted ordinary safes. Behind the jail he built a trick-shot lockbox that opens only when the target cans fall in the right order. Somebody swapped the usual tin cans for empty, licked-clean tuna cans.

Your evidence log shows the order. Follow it exactly. The range marshal will hand out eye protection and run the firing line, so do what she says, and nobody shoots until she says so.

When the lockbox opens, the last badge piece and a telegram are inside.

The badge is whole

Clue 3, from the range: Every tuna can empty. A trail of whisker-shaped footprints leading away.

Small. Furry. A tail. Lands on its feet. Loves tuna. Knocks things off tables.

Snap the three badge pieces together and set the badge in the slot on the sheriff’s box. When the box sings, press the button.

The real outlaw: Pinky “The Whisker” McGraw

She knocked the badge off the desk, dragged off the gold, and let the town blame three innocent souls. All for a lifetime supply of tuna.

The Christmas Fund is hidden at the spot marked X? on the map. Go find it. Charges against all three suspects are hereby dropped, and you are officially deputized.
